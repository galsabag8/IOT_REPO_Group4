Quick Guide:
Visualization: Click the eye to enable the 3D trail.
Calibration: Press R repeatedly until the wand appears stable (only relevant when visualization is open)
Confirm: Press Enter to lock calibration (only relevant when visualization is open)
Upload: Open a MIDI file (File button)
Start Warm‑Up: Press the wand button to begin warm‑up.
Perform: Do a full "box" motion (by the detected weight / conducting range). Music starts after warm‑up completes.
Record: Arm recording (red circle) before starting playback to save a CSV log of wand data and timing for later analysis.
Replay:Upload the original MIDI together with its CSV log to replay a recorded session visually and aurally.
